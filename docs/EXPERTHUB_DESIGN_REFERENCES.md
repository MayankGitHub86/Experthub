# ExpertHub Design References & Style Guide

## 1. Visual Theme & Aesthetics
ExpertHub combines a premium dark-mode obsidian aesthetic inspired by high-end modern SaaS and Fintech platforms (e.g. Nexora, Stripe, Linear, Raycast) with vibrant electric purple and cyan glow accents.

### Color Tokens
- **Background Primary**: `#0b0d13` (Deep Obsidian)
- **Background Secondary / Sidebar**: `#0f121d` (Dark Navy Charcoal)
- **Surface / Card Background**: `#161926`
- **Surface Hover**: `#1f2336`
- **Borders & Dividers**: `#23273b`
- **Primary Neon / Glow**: `#8b5cf6` (Vibrant Purple)
- **Secondary Neon / Accent**: `#6366f1` (Indigo Blue)
- **Cyan Accent**: `#06b6d4`
- **Success / Positive Trend**: `#10b981` (Emerald Green)
- **Warning / Pending**: `#f59e0b` (Amber)
- **Error / Danger**: `#ef4444` (Rose Red)
- **Text Primary**: `#f8fafc`
- **Text Secondary / Muted**: `#94a3b8`
- **Text Subtle**: `#64748b`

### Typography
- **Headings & Numbers**: Outfit, Inter, system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif
- **Body & Captions**: Inter, sans-serif
- **Weights**: 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (Semi-bold), 700 (Bold)
